FROM YOUR FOOTAGE TO YOUR SCENE

You choose what stays.

  1. 01

    Upload & describe

    Add your media and tell us what to preserve or leave out. Your original footage remains available for review.

  2. 02

    Review the suggestions

    The local vision model suggests source moments and styles. Check the frames, select your favorites, and save your choices.

  3. 03

    Generate & explore

    Start generation when you’re ready. Rotate your miniature, change the lighting, and download the generated 3D model.

Analysis and generation may take several minutes. Results are stylized, source-guided miniatures; faces, hidden surfaces, and geometry may differ from the original. Audio is not analyzed.
