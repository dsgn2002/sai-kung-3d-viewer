02 / Method

From video to world

All inference and scene assembly ran locally on DGX Spark. The published page serves precomputed assets; visitors do not connect to the Spark.

  1. Understand the journeySample source video frames and use Qwen3.6-27B to describe the selected Sai Kung moments.
  2. Design the sceneUse Qwen-Image-Edit-2511 to style a coast, traveler, and green-canopy boat.
  3. Generate and assembleCreate textured meshes with TRELLIS.2-4B, then place and render them in Omniverse.
  4. Explore in the browserCompress the meshes for Three.js and add animation, lighting, camera controls, and source-memory links.
03 / Output

A small world to revisit

The sample links the Sai Kung boat tour, High Island Reservoir, and MacLehose coastal trail in one artistic composition.

What the demo represents

An interactive souvenir inspired by selected video moments. The boat and water are animated, and the source frames remain available beside the scene.

Current limits

The map is an artistic composition, not surveyed geography. Hidden surfaces are generated, and the traveler is posed rather than rigged for walking animation.
