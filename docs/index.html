02 / Method

From video to world

The asset-generation models run locally on DGX Spark. The published page assembles precomputed meshes in the browser; visitors do not connect to the Spark.

  1. Understand the journeyReview source frames and record essential people, objects, clothing, and relationships in an explicit scene plan.
  2. Design the sceneUse Qwen-Image-Edit-2511 to style separate reference-conditioned people, vehicles, and environments.
  3. Generate and assembleCreate textured meshes with TRELLIS.2-4B and place the assets explicitly. The original coast was assembled in Omniverse; the revised interactive scenes are assembled in Three.js.
  4. Explore in the browserCompress the meshes for Three.js and add animation, lighting, camera controls, and source-memory links.

What the demo represents

Interactive souvenirs inspired by selected video moments. The boat, tram, and water move; source frames remain visible beside each scene. A canopy cutaway makes passenger placement easy to inspect.

Current limits

The scenes are artistic recompositions, not surveyed geography. Faces and hidden surfaces are approximate. People are posed; city pedestrians and alternate times of day are illustrative additions.
